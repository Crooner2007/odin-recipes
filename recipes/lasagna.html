Lasagna

Italian Goodness

The flavor of the best part of the mediteranean is coming to your kitchen with the lovely lasagna recipe beloved by so many in the land of amore!

Combining the creaminess of the filling with the texture of the meat and the wonderful pasta in the mix, your entire community will love this delectable dish.

Ingredients

  • Hamburger meat or sausage
  • Tomato sauce
  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozaraella cheese
  • Spices

Steps

  1. Brown the meat
  2. Boil the noodles
  3. Add the spices to the sauce
  4. Layer the ingredients
  5. Bake
